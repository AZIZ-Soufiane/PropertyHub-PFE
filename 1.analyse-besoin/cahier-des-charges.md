# Requirement Specifications: PropertyHub

## 1. Context & Objectives
- **Why this project?**: Digitalize a traditional real estate agency to eliminate manual management (spreadsheets, emails), avoid operational errors (double bookings, obsolete information), and enable scalable growth.
- **Target Audience**: 
    - **Ryan (Owner)**: For business monitoring and delegation.
    - **Michael (Agent)**: For operational efficiency in the field.
    - **Sarah (Client)**: For a smooth and modern search and booking experience.
- **Tone & Style**: Professional, Premium, Efficient, and Modern.

## 2. Functional Requirements
- **Business Need**: Centralize all real estate data and automate the client-agent connection to optimize time and maximize sales.
- **Key Features**: 
    - Centralized management of real estate listings (CRUD).
    - Advanced search system and intelligent filters for buyers.
    - Side-by-side property comparison tool.
    - 24/7 online viewing booking system with a shared calendar.
    - Integrated instant messaging to centralize exchanges.
    - Analytical dashboard for performance tracking.
- **Essential Content**: 
    - Detailed property listings (15+ HD photos, price, area, amenities, location).
    - User profiles with differentiated access rights (Admin, Agent, Client).
    - Complete history of client interactions and viewings.
    - Real-time notifications for price changes, new listings, and appointments.

## 3. Constraints
- **Deadline**: Academic Year 2025/2026.
- **Technical**: Static Site (HTML/Tailwind/Preline UI integration for the prototype) with back-end logic (Laravel 12 planned for the future).
- **Language**: English (mandatory for all technical documents and code).
