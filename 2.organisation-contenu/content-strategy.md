## Notes & Global Coherence

**Tone Applied**: 
The tone is translated through direct, action-oriented language. We avoid "fluff" and focus on efficiency and professionalism. Use words like "Instant," "Effortless," "Verified," and "Premium."

**Recurring Semantic Field**: 
"Real-time," "Centralized," "Eco-system," "Scalable," "Seamless," "Professional."

**User Journey**: 
Visitors start on the Home page (Discovery), move to Listing/Details (Engagement), and are guided to either Booking or Messaging (Conversion).
