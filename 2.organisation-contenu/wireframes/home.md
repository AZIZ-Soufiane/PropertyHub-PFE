# Wireframe: Home Page

## ZONE 1: Header (Navigation)
- **Component**: Navbar
- **Content**: Logo "PropertyHub", Menu (Home, Properties, Compare, Dashboard), Primary CTA [Login]
- **Action**: Navigation to respective pages

## ZONE 2: Hero (Search)
- **Component**: Hero Section
- **Content**: H1 "Find Your Perfect Property with Effortless Management", Sub-headline "Search, compare, and book in seconds.", Search Bar (Location, Type, Price Range)
- **Action**: Search results redirection to Listing page

## ZONE 3: Featured Listings (Engagement)
- **Component**: Card Grid
- **Content**: H2 "Featured Properties", Top 3 Trending listings (Price, Image, Beds/Baths)
- **Action**: Click card to visit Property Details

## ZONE 4: Value Proposition (Trust)
- **Component**: Icon Grid
- **Content**: H2 "Why Choose PropertyHub?", 3 Blocks: [Instant Booking], [Verified Data], [Unified Platform]
- **Action**: None (Informational)

## ZONE 5: Footer (Utility)
- **Component**: Footer
- **Content**: Links, Social Icons, Copyright "PropertyHub 2026"
- **Action**: External links/Legal pages
