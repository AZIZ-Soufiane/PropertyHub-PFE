# 🗺️ Empathy Map – PropertyHub Real Estate Management System

**Date:** February 26, 2026 | **Project:** PropertyHub

---

## 👤 **ADMIN - Property Manager (Ryan)**

### 👂 What He HEARS
- Property appointment requests from clients
- Complaints about time wasted managing properties manually
- "When will we get the statistics?"
- Calls to confirm property visits

### 👁️ What He SEES
- Stacks of property documents
- Unorganized appointment requests
- Complex Excel spreadsheets for statistics

### 🧠 What He THINKS and FEELS
**Thinks:** "I'm doing work that a system should automate."  
**Thinks:** "How can I grow with this manual management?"  
**Feels:** Stress about not forgetting anything  
**Feels:** Desire for complete control over inventory

### 🗣️ What He SAYS and DOES
**Says:** "I have to approve each appointment manually."  
**Says:** "Statistics are never up to date."  
**Does:** Creates, edits, and deletes listings  
**Does:** Manages appointment requests

### 🎯 PAINS and GAINS
🔴 **PAINS**
- Time-consuming manual property management
- Difficulty tracking client appointments
- Lack of visibility into performance
- Risk of missing important requests

🟢 **GAINS**
- Centralized and comprehensive dashboard
- Automated appointment management
- Real-time statistics
- Reduced administrative burden

---

## 👤 **CLIENT - Property Seeker**

### 👂 What They HEAR
- "How many bedrooms does this property have?"
- "When can I visit?"
- Friend recommendations
- New listing notifications

### 👁️ What They SEE
- Endless lists of properties
- Variable quality images
- Listings without adequate filters

### 🧠 What They THINK and FEEL
**Think:** "Where can I find the right property for me?"  
**Think:** "How can I compare quickly?"  
**Feel:** Excitement about finding a property  
**Feel:** Overwhelmed by too many options

### 🗣️ What They SAY and DO
**Say:** "I want to search by location, price, and number of bedrooms."  
**Say:** "How can I compare properties?"  
**Do:** Browse listings and filter  
**Do:** Save favorites and schedule visits

### 🎯 PAINS and GAINS
🔴 **PAINS**
- Too many properties to browse manually
- Insufficient or poorly organized filters
- Difficulty comparing listings
- Long waits for visit confirmations

🟢 **GAINS**
- Fast search with criteria filters
- Visual presentation with multiple images
- Easy property comparison
- Save favorites for later review
